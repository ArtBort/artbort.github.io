About

I'm Bart Lefèvre, and I make my work under the name Bort_. I have been making collage since I was 18, and today I build it in layers and in three dimensions.

I live and work in Zelzate, Belgium. Each piece is made by hand, and I stream the whole making process live on Twitch, so you can watch a piece take shape and ask questions as it happens.
