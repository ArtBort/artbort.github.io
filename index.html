Streaming art on the internet

In 2017, I started sharing my creation process by streaming live on the internet. To be specific, on Twitch.com, at the time I lived in Belgium in a small caravan. It was a place where I could let my feelings flow and share them with others. A few strokes of glue to put everything together, and we had our own place. Where creative people came to share and enjoy the creative process. It's strange what effect a few old photos can have on you. At this point the live stream has become a place where I like to start from. To motivate me to do all the little things that make me happy and share them with whoever is interested.

Recycled materials

To me, there was always something special in seeing value in things that people discard. The overconsumption is massive. There is a lot of material available to work with. One of my favourite easy-to-find materials is cardboard; it's strong, and it has nice tones of brown. To be honest, my absolute favourite resource material is old magazines, from the 1920s in particular. Although 70s magazines are also up there. The message is: the material is out there! It has its own beauty; you just have to see it.

Surrealism

I have always been a surrealist at heart. When I first saw a surreal painting, I was lost in a world of imagination; it never really left me. When I saw the work of a Dadaist for the first time, I was very confused. It broke all the rules; it triggered my brain in a way that I had not encountered before. The freedom to create something that is unique and not bound by rules.
