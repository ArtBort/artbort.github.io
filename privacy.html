Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This website

This website shows the artwork of Bort_ (Bart Lefèvre). It does not use tracking cookies or analytics. If you send an order or contact request through the form, your name, email and message are only used to answer you and handle your order. They are not sold or shared with anyone else.

Bort_ Post Hub

Bort_ Post Hub is a private tool that Bort_ uses on his own computer to post his own art photos and videos to his own social media accounts, including YouTube. When it connects to a Google account, it only uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and set their titles and descriptions on that account. It does not collect, store on any server, sell or share data from any Google account. Login tokens stay on the computer running the app, and access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Bort_ Post Hub's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
